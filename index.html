V5: Real 3D Printable Models

V5 Approach: Generated Funko Pop-style character art from reference photos (8-9/10 likeness), then built actual 3D printable STL geometry — oversized head, tapered body, arms, legs, base stand. Full 3D mesh, not standees or billboards. Three.js viewer below lets you rotate, zoom, and inspect each model. STL download links for 3D printing.
